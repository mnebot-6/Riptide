## Full description (4000 chars max)

Riptide turns your daily habits into a living marine ecosystem that grows with your consistency.

Every task you complete earns experience for your sea creatures. The more consistent you are, the more your ocean thrives — new species appear, creatures evolve, and your underwater world becomes richer and more beautiful.

**How it works:**
- Create work blocks (Study, Exercise, Work, etc.) and add tasks to each one
- Complete your tasks throughout the day
- Every night, Riptide evaluates your progress and rewards your marine ecosystem
- Unlock new creatures through a lootbox system with 5 rarity tiers
- Watch your creatures swim, grow, and evolve with beautiful Canvas animations

**What makes Riptide different:**
- No scores, no rankings, no pressure. Your progress is shown through emotional feedback and visual growth — never as a number
- Missing a day pauses your ecosystem. It never goes backward. Your ocean waits for you
- 70 unique marine species across 10 categories, each with hand-drawn Canvas animations
- Your data stays on your device first. Cloud sync is optional

**Features:**
- Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, monthly) with flexible scheduling
- Countable tasks, timers, priorities, and markdown notes
- Night summary with personalized emotional messages
- Statistics with weekly/monthly charts and streak tracking
- History view with 30/60/90 day filters
- Home screen widget with today's tasks and progress
- Live wallpaper — your aquarium as your phone background
- Push notifications for task reminders and daily summaries
- Full offline support with optional cloud sync
- Available in English and Spanish

Riptide is for anyone who wants to build better habits without the anxiety of streaks, scores, or competition. Just you and your ocean, growing together.
