Riptide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Summary: Riptide is a personal productivity app. We collect minimal data, we don't sell anything, and we don't track you. Your tasks and ecosystem live on your device first.

1. What data we collect

Riptide collects only what is strictly necessary for the app to function:

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, we store your email address, display name, and profile picture URL to identify your account.
  • Task data: Your work blocks, tasks, recurring task definitions, daily summaries, streaks, ecosystem state, and marine creatures. This data is created by you and stored primarily on your device.
  • Sync data: If you enable cloud sync, your task data is transmitted to our server to keep your devices in sync. Timestamps are used for conflict resolution.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts or phone data
  • No third-party trackers or SDKs
  • No crash reporting services

3. How data is stored

  • On your device: All data is stored locally using an encrypted database (Room). The app works fully offline.
  • On our server: If you enable sync, data is stored on a secure PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway (EU region). Communication uses HTTPS encryption.

4. Authentication

Riptide uses Google Sign-In for authentication. We receive a token from Google to verify your identity. We do not have access to your Google password. Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored securely on your device and refreshed automatically.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.

Your data is only used to provide the Riptide service to you.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Your local data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Server-side data is retained while your account is active.
  • To delete all your data, you can sign out from the app (which removes local tokens) and contact us to request full server-side deletion.

7. Children's privacy

Riptide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
